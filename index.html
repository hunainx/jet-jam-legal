1. Overview

Jet Jam (com.hunainx.jetjam) is a single-player mobile puzzle game. Your level progress, coins, stars, airport upgrades, and settings are saved locally on your device. We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise GPS location, camera, or microphone data.

We integrate third-party SDKs (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics, and Unity In-App Purchasing with Google Play Billing) to serve ads, manage consent, process optional in-app purchases, and collect pseudonymous gameplay analytics and crash diagnostics after consent.

3. Managing Your Consent & Choices

  • In-App Consent Manager: On first launch in applicable regions, a UMP consent screen is shown before any ad request or analytics initialization. You can update your choice anytime at Settings → Privacy options.
  • Android Advertising ID: You may reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Target Audience & Children's Privacy

Jet Jam is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data without parental consent, contact us at hunainibnshakeel@gmail.com for prompt deletion.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

You can delete all local game data immediately using Settings → Reset Progress or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of pseudonymous analytics or crash records held by our service providers, email hunainibnshakeel@gmail.com with the subject Jet Jam — Data Deletion Request.
